WHY 背景

What the system solves系統解決之問題

  • Know who is inside the stand / venue at all times.隨時掌握看台／場館內之學生人數與名單。
  • Every exit (toilet / tuck shop) recorded with time, duration and gate.每次外出(廁所／小食部)均記錄時間、時長與閘口。
  • Phones kept in numbered slots — one phone, one slot, one record.手機存放於編號格位——一機一格一記錄。
  • Absences over the threshold are flagged automatically.超過時限之外出自動標記,便於跟進。

EXIT 外出

Exit flow in four steps外出流程四步

  • Student scans the QR code on the student card.學生掃描學生證上之 QR 碼。
  • Prefect taps the purpose: Toilet / Food / Competition / Helper / Other.風紀點選目的:廁所／小食部／參賽／工作人員／其他。
  • System checks lists & houses, then shows a traffic signal.系統核對名單與社別,然後顯示放行信號。
  • Phone goes into the displayed slot (not required for Competition / Helper).手機放入顯示之格位(參賽／工作人員無需交出手機)。
    • RETURN 返回

    Return flow返回流程

  • Student scans again at the gate (Competition / Helper: any gate).學生再次掃碼(參賽／工作人員可於任何閘口返回)。
  • Screen shows time outside and the slot number for phone collection.畫面顯示在外時間與取回手機之格位編號。
  • Over the threshold? Yellow caution + record flagged.超過時限?顯示黃色注意信號並於記錄標記。
    • SIGNALS 信號

    Read the light, act at once看清信號,即時行動

    PASS 放行

    Procedure complete — admit the student.手續完成,可讓學生通過。

    STOP 禁止通過

    Wrong gate · slots full · already out · not found · not on list.錯閘口・格位已滿・重複外出・查無此人・不在名單。

    CAUTION 注意

    Overlong return · previously overlong · no exit record · phone at another gate.返回超時・曾超時・無外出記錄・手機於其他閘口。

    CUSTODY 保管

    Phone slots & channels手機格與通道

    12 slots per gate 每閘 12 格

    Full = exits blocked. Tap a slot for occupant details; Manual Return (password) releases it.滿格即暫停外出;點選格位可查占用人資料;「手動返回」(需密碼)釋放格位。

    Two channel types 兩種通道

    Normal: all purposes + slots. Special: Competition/Helper only, no slots, no limit.普通:所有目的加手機格。專用:僅參賽/工作人員,無手機格、無限時。

    Two houses per gate 每閘兩社

    Other houses get STOP — except Competition/Helper, exempt everywhere.其他社顯示禁止——參賽/工作人員於任何閘口豁免。

    Overlong ≥ threshold 超時

    Flagged on return; next exit shows a caution badge; slots flash red.返回時標記;下次外出顯示注意標記;格位紅色閃爍。
